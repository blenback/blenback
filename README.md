### Hi there, I'm

# Ben Black.

Head of the Working Group **[Data &amp; Modelling Infrastructure for Living Labs](https://iat-dml.github.io/)**, at ZALF.

A scientific researcher working on spatial modelling, data and scientific visualisation.<br>
I share the work that doesn't always make it into papers — code, maps and field notes — in the hope that it's useful.
